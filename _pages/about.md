I am Aswin Ravikumar Rangasamy Veerasamy (in case you are curious, my name comes with built-in ancestry), a first-year CS PhD Student at [School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence, Arizona State University](https://nlp.asu.edu/research/). I am advised by Prof. [Chitta Baral](https://search.asu.edu/profile/231973) at the [COGINT Lab](https://cogintlab-asu.github.io/) and also mentored by Prof. [Ben Zhou](https://search.asu.edu/profile/5107148). Currently, my research focuses on reasoning-aware training approaches inspired by human-interactive learning scenarios. Throughout history, humans have become more intelligent by expanding knowledge and understanding through interaction with one another. Similarly, I believe that training models through human-inspired interactive learning approaches would expand their reasoning capabilities.

Previously, I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Science at Arizona State University in Summer, 2025. Prior to that, I obtained my Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science at [College of Engineering, Guindy, Anna University](https://ceg.annauniv.edu/).
